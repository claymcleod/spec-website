An expression is a compound statement that consists of literal values, identifiers (references to [declarations](@/1.3/expressions/declarations.md) or call outputs), [built-in operators](@/1.3/expressions/operators.md) (e.g., `+` or `>=`), and calls to standard library functions. This section covers all aspects of WDL expressions, including declarations, operators, member access, string interpolation, and more.
